Section 01

Event Overview

Car Modification Fighter 2026 is an ambitious, full-scale automotive showcase organised by KJ Garage — one of Malaysia's most active and progressive car modification workshops, established in 2023.

The event brings together car owners, automotive clubs, vendors, industry brands and the general public for a comprehensive day of competitions, live performances, exhibitions and community celebration at AEON Mall Nilai.

KJ Garage has already established its credibility on the national circuit, having participated in prestigious events including Retro Havoc, Tokyo Auto Salon Malaysia, Motor Maniac, Aramaitii Motorsport, Cpurt Event Show, I AM KL, and BKGK Show.

"This event is not just a car show — it's a strategic platform where automotive passion intersects with real business opportunity. With 10,000 expected visitors, sponsors gain access to Malaysia's most engaged automotive audience in a single day."
